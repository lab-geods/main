GEO-DS / The laboratory

The laboratory

We connect geological knowledge with data acquisition and analysis, from field observation to interpretation.

01 / THE LABORATORY

The Earth is our
starting point.

GEO-DS (Geological Data Science) is a laboratory at the School of Sciences and Technology, University of Évora. It brings together research, education and specialist services, with links to the Institute of Earth Sciences.

From the Alentejo to other regions, we combine field observations, digital surveys and data analysis to study geological and environmental systems.

01Research and interpret

Combining geological, geochemical and geospatial data to investigate mineral resources, materials and environmental processes.

02Teach and share

Supporting education, training and science communication by connecting surveying and analytical technologies with their applications.

03Collaborate and transfer

Specialist services and collaboration with public and private organisations, from digital documentation to data interpretation.

An integrated approach.

Three parts of the same research process.

01

In the field

We plan surveys around the scientific question, site conditions and observation scale. Field records provide context and guide subsequent processing.

02

In the data

We organise geometric, spectral and geochemical information into coherent workflows. Recording the methods helps people understand the outputs and their limitations.

03

In the knowledge

We interpret results alongside available geological and historical evidence. Maps, models and publications make data useful for research, teaching and collaboration.

Research, teaching and collaboration.

From the University of Évora to concrete questions about the landscape.

The laboratory brings together fieldwork, materials analysis and digital technologies. This connection supports geoscientific questions and the documentation of environments and heritage.

GEO-DS brings together different training and research backgrounds. Sharing methods, discussing results and preparing data products are part of the team’s everyday work.
