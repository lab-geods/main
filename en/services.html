What we can develop.

Each commission is scoped around its objective, location and required scale.

01

Three-dimensional surveys

Recording terrain, buildings and heritage sites using airborne and terrestrial methods. Point clouds are integrated and organised for documentation and analysis.

Point clouds · 3D models · DTM / DSM

02

Mapping and spatial information

Preparation of mapping, orthophotos and organised information for consultation and integration into geographic information systems.

Orthophotos · Mapping · GIS data

03

Spectral characterisation

Acquisition and processing of multispectral and hyperspectral information, interpreted in relation to the materials and objectives of the work.

Spectral data · Indices · Thematic maps

04

Processing and technical support

Integration of data from different sources, quality control and preparation of outputs and technical reports that put the results in context.

Processing · Integration · Reports

How to start.

An initial conversation helps define the work.

  1. 01

    Scope

    Share the objective, location, approximate area and the products you need.

  2. 02

    Define

    The team reviews methods, acquisition conditions and an appropriate level of detail.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Data acquisition and processing, checks and preparation of the agreed outputs.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Data and contextual information to help you use and understand the results.
