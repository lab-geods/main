Research in context.

Examples connecting data, materials and landscapes.

In mining areas, geological observation, spectroscopy and geochemistry support the study of materials and element distributions. Work at Trimpancho illustrates this approach and has published results.

At archaeological sites and in buildings, three-dimensional recording supports the documentation of shapes, spatial relationships and surfaces. The atlas offers examples from different locations and acquisition methods.
