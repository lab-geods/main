From survey to product.

A data product should be accompanied by the information needed to use it.

Products and interpretation
Product What it represents What to document
Point cloud Three-dimensional geometry of terrain and structures. Registration, alignment, density, colours and classification.
DTM / DSM Representation of terrain and surface elevations. Reference system, resolution, classification and interpolation.
3D model Representation of surfaces and objects. Coverage, detail, scale and processing documentation.
Orthophoto Spatially referenced imagery for consultation and mapping. Resolution, georeferencing and the quality of visible areas.
Spectral data Comparison of material responses across spectral bands. Calibration, acquisition conditions and contextual interpretation.

Quality in context.

More detail does not replace acquisition suited to the objective.

Scale and acquisition conditions influence the result. Before a survey, we define its objective, area, level of detail and intended outputs.

Processing includes checks of alignment, coverage and data consistency. Final outputs should identify the reference system where applicable and the main processing steps.
